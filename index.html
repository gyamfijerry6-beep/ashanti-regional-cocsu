Weekly administrator checklist







Suggested Digital Engagement Team

Form a small team of 3–5 volunteers to prepare content, welcome new members, coordinate quizzes, review submissions and publicise regional programmes.

Group guidelines

  1. Keep posts relevant to COCSU and Christian fellowship.
  2. Be respectful when opinions differ.
  3. Do not spam or share unverified information.
  4. Get consent before posting members' photos or personal details.
  5. Use approved administrators for official announcements.
